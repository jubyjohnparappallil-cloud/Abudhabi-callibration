# SEO Strategy — Abu Dhabi Calibration

## 1. Goal
Rank the website on Google for calibration-related searches in Abu Dhabi and the UAE, so potential clients find the business organically (without paying for ads).

## 2. Target Keywords (Keyword Research)

| Priority | Keyword | Type | Target Page |

| High | calibration services Abu Dhabi | Commercial | Home / Services |
| High | calibration company UAE | Commercial | Home |

## 3. On-Page SEO (per page)
For every page and post:
- Unique **SEO title** (55–60 characters) containing the focus keyword
- **Meta description** (150–160 characters) with the keyword and a call to action
- One **H1** heading, logical **H2/H3** structure
- **Focus keyword** used naturally in the first paragraph, a heading, and image alt text
- **Alt text** on every image
- **Internal links** to related services/pages
- Clean URL slug (e.g. `/pressure-calibration/`)

## 4. Technical SEO (already built into the theme)
- Mobile responsive, fast-loading
- LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema
- Geo meta tags (Abu Dhabi)
- XML sitemap (via Rank Math)
- HTTPS / SSL
- Clean heading hierarchy

## 5. Off-Page SEO (Backlinks / Link Building)
Backlinks = other websites linking to ours. Plan:
- Register on **Google Business Profile** (most important for local SEO)
- List in UAE business directories (Yellow Pages UAE, Connect.ae, etc.)
- Industry/partner websites and supplier pages
- Guest articles on calibration/industrial blogs linking back
- Social profiles (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram) linking to the site

## 6. Content Marketing
- Publish blog posts targeting informational keywords (already 25 articles prepared)
- Update posts regularly; add new topics monthly
- Answer common customer questions (FAQ page + blog)

## 7. Tools Used
- **Google Search Console** — indexing, keyword performance
- **Google Analytics** — traffic and behaviour
- **Rank Math** (WordPress plugin) — on-page SEO scoring, sitemaps, schema
- **Google Business Profile** — local search & maps
